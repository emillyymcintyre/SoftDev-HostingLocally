These are my projects!

Potions is a game where you jump around to collect materials and then eventually put those materials into a potion that you make! Each potion has different effects, which different NPS's want. These NPCs will give you a request and you must give them the effect from the potion that they want!

Jumpy House is a shmup game where you play as a little bunny defending your house. Press x to shoot and then c to release a big shot, the only shot capable of defeating the boss snail.

Coffeeria is a Papa's game inspired coffee shop where you receive a customers order and then choose ingredients to make a cup of coffee for that customer. You can fail or get it right.
